Vizianagaram (విజయనగరం)

"The City of Victory" — The cultural cradle of northern Andhra where royal Gajapati citadels stand beside the monumental celestial bow of Ramanarayanam and master woodcarvers shape the Bobbili Veena.

Fast Facts

Getting to Vizianagaram

Conveniently situated just an hour north of Visakhapatnam along NH-26.

Nearest Airport

Visakhapatnam Airport (VTZ) is approximately 55 km south. Direct taxi or cab takes around 1 hour 15 minutes via four-lane highway NH-16 / NH-26.

Railway Hub

Vizianagaram Junction (VZM) is one of the busiest junction stations on the East Coast Railway, connecting Howrah, Chennai, Raipur, and Hyderabad.

Road Transit

Well-served by APSRTC non-stop and express buses leaving every 15 minutes from Vizag RTC complex to Vizianagaram.

Sirimanotsavam

Every year in October (after Dussehra), the town hosts the world-famous Sirimanotsavam festival honoring Goddess Pydi Thalli with royal processions.

Architectural Wonder

Ramanarayanam Spiritual Bow Park

Constructed precisely in the shape of Sri Rama's celestial bow (Kodanda) and arrow, Ramanarayanam spans 15 acres. A two-story air-conditioned pathway forms the curved bow, exhibiting over 160 sculpted 3D scenes from Valmiki's Ramayana.

  • Aerial Bow Configuration: Spectacular view from high vantage points.
  • Evening Laser & Light Show: Grand musical water fountain dance after sunset.
  • Veda Pathashala: Preserving ancient Vedic chanting traditions.

Living GI Heritage

The Legendary Bobbili Veena

Since the 17th century, master artisans in the Gollapalli village of Bobbili have carved the prestigious Ekanda Veena from a single solid block of seasoned Jackfruit wood. The single-piece construction delivers a crystalline acoustic resonance that Carnatic masters consider unmatched.

GI Tag Recognition The Bobbili Veena was awarded the Geographical Indication (GI) tag in 2011 to preserve this 300-year-old unbroken royal craftsmanship.
