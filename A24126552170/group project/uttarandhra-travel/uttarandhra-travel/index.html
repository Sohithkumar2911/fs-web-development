Essential Travel Tips

Best Time to Visit the Coast

The golden period to travel across Visakhapatnam, Vizianagaram, and Srikakulam is from October to March. Enjoy cool ocean breezes, mild daytime temperatures (18°C – 28°C), and colorful regional celebrations such as the Sirimanotsavam and Ratha Saptami.

VTZ Airport Daily flights to all metros
NH-16 & Rail Smooth 4-lane expressway

Quick Travel Advice

  • Attire: Modest traditional attire (dhoti, saree, or kurta) is appreciated when entering Simhachalam, Arasavalli, and Sri Kurmam temples.
  • Vistadome Booking: Book your Vizag to Araku train tickets at least 3-4 weeks in advance on IRCTC to secure the glass-domed observatory seats.
  • Artisan Souvenirs: Support local master artisans by purchasing authentic GI Ponduru Khadi and miniature Bobbili Veenas directly at source.
