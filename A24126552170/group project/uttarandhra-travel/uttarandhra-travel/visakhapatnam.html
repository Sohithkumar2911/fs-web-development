Visakhapatnam (విశాఖపట్నం)

"The City of Destiny" — Where sapphire ocean breakers wash along scenic beach roads, ancient Buddhist viharas look down from emerald clifftops, and scenic hill railways climb to misty valleys.

Fast Facts

Connectivity & Essentials

Everything you need to seamlessly navigate the premier port city of Andhra Pradesh.

By Air

Visakhapatnam Airport (VTZ) connects daily with nonstop flights to Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, and direct flights to Singapore & Kuala Lumpur.

By Rail

Visakhapatnam Junction (VSKP) is an A1 super-grade station on the Howrah-Chennai mainline, equipped with Vande Bharat Express and tourist Vistadome coaches.

By Road

NH-16 (Golden Quadrilateral) passes straight through the city. Regular luxury AC Volvo buses operate from RTC Complex to Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Vizianagaram, and Srikakulam.

Best Season

October through March: Balmy coastal weather (20°C to 28°C), perfect for morning surf sessions, hilltop cable car rides, and open-air beach dining.

Eastern Ghats Hill Escape

The Araku Valley Vistadome Experience

Just 110 km from Vizag city center lies Araku Valley, perched at 3,000 feet above sea level. Board the famous Vizag-Kirandul Passenger with its glass-roof Vistadome coach to cruise through 58 mountain tunnels, deep gorges, and terraced coffee plantations.

  • Borra Caves: Subterranean 1-million-year-old limestone karst formations.
  • Araku Organic Coffee: World-renowned GI-certified Arabica coffee estates.
  • Bongu Bamboo Chicken: Traditional wood-fired tribal delicacy cooked without oil.
